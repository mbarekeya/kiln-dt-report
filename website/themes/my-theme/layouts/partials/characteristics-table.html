Characteristics Description
C1 System under study Lumber drying operations of a sawmill: green boards are grouped into packages at the stacking line, stored in the yard, and dried in five batch kilns.
C2 Physical acting components heater_coils: Heating coils raising the dry-bulb temperature according to the drying schedule
fans: Fans circulating hot air through the load; direction reverses periodically
dampers: Vents evacuating moist air from the kiln during the drying phases
C3 Physical sensing components mc_meter: Handheld moisture meter used for the manual MC tests near the end of the cycle and after drying
th_sensor: Wet-bulb temperature sensor of the kiln; its data stay in the kiln controller and are not yet transmitted to the DT
production_monitoring_system: Production monitoring system recording each package as it leaves the stacking line (dimension, length, pieces, production date), updated every hour
ts_sensor: Dry-bulb temperature sensor of the kiln; its data stay in the kiln controller and are not yet transmitted to the DT
C4 Physical-to-virtual interaction mc_tests_trans: Results of the manual MC tests, recorded in the kiln log
kiln_log_trans: Loads recorded manually by operators after drying: loading and drying times, loaded length and volume, length lost
package_records_trans: Package records collected automatically from the production monitoring system
C5 Virtual-to-physical interaction Manual: loading plans and drying recommendations are applied by operators. No command is sent to the kiln.
C6 DT services mc_estimation_service: Estimates the MC of the load from the dry-bulb and wet-bulb temperatures and compares it with the planned MC
visualization_3d_service: 3D view of the kiln reproducing the arrangement of the validated load
monitoring_service: Web dashboard to request and validate loading plans, follow the drying cycle, and review recommendations
kiln_loading_optimization_service: Builds the available inventory from the package records and computes one loading plan per lumber dimension with a lexicographic MILP
C7 Twinning time-scale No data available
C8 Multiplicities One DT covering the stacking line, the yard, and one studied kiln; extension to the five kilns of the sawmill is planned.
C9 Life-cycle stages Operation stage: preparation of kiln loads and follow-up of the drying cycle.
C10 DT models and data Model:
kiln_3d_model: 3D scene of the kiln with lanes and lumber packages
loading_milp_model: Column-based MILP: columns of four packages of the same length placed in lanes; maximizes the loaded volume, then prioritizes the oldest packages
drying_model: Falling-rate drying kinetics dMC/dt = -k(MC - EMC) with Hailwood-Horrobin EMC; k not yet calibrated
drying_schedule_model: Drying schedules of the sawmill for spruce and jack pine, target MC 15 %

Data:
kiln_log_data: Kiln log of the sawmill, used to analyse losses and over-drying and, in future work, to calibrate the drying model
simulated_temperature_data: Dry-bulb and wet-bulb temperatures generated by the simulator and sent to the MC estimation service; will be replaced by the measured temperatures once the kiln controllers are accessible
package_data: Package records stored by the collector and used to build the inventory
C11 Tooling and enablers rabbit_mq: RabbitMQ AMQP broker; services communicate only through it (RPC queues)
simulator: Kiln simulator following the drying schedules of the sawmill; replaces the kiln controllers until they are accessible
docker: Docker Compose; one container per service and one for the broker
godot: client for the 3D view of the kiln
fastapi: Python FastAPI web server of the monitoring dashboard; relays operator requests to the services through RabbitMQ
highs: HiGHS MILP solver called through SciPy
production_data_collector: Collects the package records from the production monitoring system of the sawmill
C12 DT constellation Services in Docker containers communicating only through RabbitMQ; Godot client on the host machine; production data collected from the sawmill production monitoring system.
C13 Twinning process and DT evolution Inventory and loading side: digital shadow (automatic flow from the plant, manual return). Kiln side: digital model (simulated data). Connection to the kiln controllers is planned to close both loops.
C14 Fidelity and validity considerations Inventory built from production records, without transfers between yards. Drying constant k not calibrated, since the MC is not measured during drying. MC model not validated against measured data.
C15 DT technical connection Automatic collection of package records from the production monitoring system; AMQP (RabbitMQ) between services; no connection to the kiln controllers yet.
C16 DT hosting/deployment Deployed locally with Docker Compose.
C17 Insights and decision making estimated_mc: Estimated MC and its deviation from the planned MC during the drying cycle
cycle_report: Report generated at the end of the cycle
monitoring_dashboard: Dashboard showing the loading plans, MC curves, drying progress, and recommendations
drying_recommendation: Recommendations to the kiln operator, who can review or override them; no command is sent to the kiln
loading_plans: One loading plan per dimension with its loaded volume, lane fill, and mean package age
validated_load: Loading plan validated by the planner and applied manually in the yard
kiln_3d_view: 3D view of the validated load inside the kiln
C18 Horizontal integration Integrated with the production monitoring system of the sawmill; not yet with the planning system or the kiln controllers.
C19 Data ownership and privacy Production and kiln data belong to the industrial partner and are used within an academic collaboration; the partner is not named.
C20 Standardization Architecture aligned with ISO 23247; communication via AMQP (RabbitMQ) with JSON payloads.
C21 Security and safety considerations No physical safety risk: no command is sent to the kiln, and all decisions remain with the operators.
